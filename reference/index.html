    How it works

    1. Set it up for you

      Choose your language, reading level, text size and colours. This takes under a minute and is remembered on your device.

    2. Speak, type or show

      Ask a question, paste a letter, add a photo, or turn on captions for a conversation.

    3. Get it your way

      Read a short answer, or have it read aloud. Ask again in simpler words as many times as you like.
